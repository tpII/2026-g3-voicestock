## Procedure

No practice cycle. Procedure version 1 allows one and does not require it.

Three measured cycles. In each cycle the operator:

1. Held the button past the configured two-second limit.
2. Waited until `Capture completed` printed `Termination: MAX_DURATION`.
3. Kept holding for about two more seconds.
4. Saw no additional capture while the button stayed pressed.
5. Released.
6. Pressed again, recorded briefly, and released before the limit.
7. Saw a new file with `Termination: RELEASED`.

The continued hold after `MAX_DURATION` is the operator's direct
observation. It is not an instrumented measurement of how long the
controller stayed in the waiting state. That interval is **NOT MEASURED**.

The switch is a simple momentary button. A second independent mechanical
press cannot be made while the same switch stays down. Criterion 3 is
**NOT APPLICABLE** for all three cycles. The reason is: second press not
mechanically possible with the physical switch while held. That is not a
failed behavior. This record does not claim that the software received a
second press event during an active recording. The controller event queue
was not instrumented. The protection observed here is the absence of an
extra WAV while the button stayed physically down after the forced stop.
